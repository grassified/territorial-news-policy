# TERRITORIAL.IO NEWS STATION CONSTITUTION 📰
> *Open. Transparent. Fair.*

> **A framework built for creators: protecting journalistic freedom, eliminating staff bias and building a transparent news ecosystem**.
## 💼 I. DUTIES & FREEDOM OF JOURNALISM
- **Factual Reporting**: All News Stations added within this server shall report news in a factual and evidence-based manner. Every journalist must make reasonable efforts to verify facts and evidence prior to publication.
- **Community Rumors & Unverified Reports**: News stations are permitted to cover community discussions, leaks and unverified rumors; provided they include a mandatory disclaimer below the article title, similar to this:
`⚠️ UNVERIFIED REPORT: This article covers community rumors/speculation. No official evidence has been verified.`
- **Right of Response & Transparency**: When covering controversial community disputes or major clan developments; journalists are not required to seek approval prior to reporting, but are required to reach out to involved parties for comment. If a party declines or fails to comment, the article must explicitly note that no response was provided from that party:
`Reached out to [Party] for comment; no response was provided prior to publication.`
- **Journalistic Immunity**: This policy guarantees immunity from arbitrary punishments for simply performing legitimate journalistic duties.
> *Exception*: Immunity is revoked if an article or station violates [Territorial.io Terms](https://territorial.io/terms), [General Server Rules](https://discord.com/channels/991752805213802516/1547670987913429022) or [News Policy](https://discord.com/channels/991752805213802516/1553096767191519373).

## ⛔ II. CODE OF CONDUCT & CORE STANDARDS
### 1. Defamation & Neutrality
- **NO News Station or Author** shall spread false or unverified information intended to harm an individual or group's reputation.
- Any article containing subjective opinions, political analysis or editorial commentary **MUST include a disclaimer at the footer**:
`This is an editorial article and may contain personal opinion.`
### 2. Constructive Debate vs. Malicious "Beefing"
- **Prohibited**: Bad-faith slander, personal harassment, slurs, targeted threats or deliberate provocations against individuals or rival stations.
- **Allowed & Encouraged**: Respectful, evidence-based criticism, factual debunks and analytical rebuttals regarding public actions or clan events.
### 3. Promotions & Allowed Links
- Direct advertising of external Discord servers, giveaways, recruitment spam, bots or products is strictly prohibited.
- **Allowed Links**: Neutral articles informing the community about community-hosted events; as well as safe, open-source community tools/utilities (e.g, stats trackers or map tools that comply with Territorial.io Terms) are explicitly permitted.
- **Archive Link Exemption**: Independent (non-clan) stations may include **one small masked link** at the footer pointing directly to their publication archive or Discord server. Clan-run stations are not permitted to include invite links.
### 4. Relevancy
- All articles published in [Territorial News](https://discord.com/channels/991752805213802516/1546928898460352573) must be directly relevant to **Territorial.io**, clan politics, community events, in-game updates or open-source community utilities. Small mentions or comparisons to other games are permitted as reference points, provided the main topic of the article isn't centered around that game.
## 💬 III. THREAD & READER ENGAGEMENT RULES
To allow readers to engage with articles while keeping the main channels clean, journalists may create discussion threads under strict guidelines:
### 1. Strict 1-Thread Limit Per Article
Journalists are permitted to create **at most ONE public discussion thread per published article**.
### 2. Mandatory Article Tie-In
The thread **must** be created directly under the relevant published article and share its title.
### 3. No Standalone/Spam Threads
Creating threads without an accompanying published article, opening off-topic chatter threads or creating duplicate threads is strictly prohibited.
### 4. Defined Moderation Triggers
News Managers and staff reserve the right to lock, archive or delete discussion threads if:
- Toxicity escalates beyond constructive debate (violating server moderation rules).
- Spam or bot activity overpowers genuine reader discussion.
- The author abandons the thread for 7+ days without addressing key community inquiries.
> *Note: Repeated thread spam will result in Territorial Journalist privileges being revoked.*

## ⭐ IV. CLAN AFFILIATION & TRANSPARENCY
- **Mandatory Disclosure**: Any news station owned, operated or affiliated with a clan or group **MUST include a disclaimer on every article published**:
`⚠️ This news station is affiliated with [Clan Name].`
- **Balanced Content Rule (1:2 Ratio)**: Clan-affiliated stations must maintain a minimum ratio of **at least 2 general/community news articles for every 1 clan-focused article**. Stations using their slot purely for clan self-glorification or recruitment propaganda will face review.
- **Equal Expectations**: Clan stations submit to the exact same quality and conduct expectations as independent stations.

## 🎨 V. QUALITY, STRUCTURAL STANDARDS & STYLING GUIDE
- **Styling Guide Compliance**: Articles must adhere to the official **News Styling Guide** (pinned in [Territorial News](https://discord.com/channels/991752805213802516/1546928898460352573), with an optional extended guide document); maintaining clear paragraph spacing, proper headers, sound English grammar and verifiable context links or screenshots for major factual claims.
- **Dual Feedback & Removal Process**: If **2 News Managers** agree an article fails to meet structural standards, it may be removed.
- News Managers **must** log the action in the appropriate logs thread, forward it to the appropriate channel and provide written constructive feedback to **both the journalist who wrote the article and their station owner/head**.

## 🎭 VI. SATIRE NEWS POLICY
- **Dedicated Channel**: Satire and humor articles are permitted exclusively in [Satire News](https://discord.com/channels/991752805213802516/1546928907503280241).
- **Minimum Article Structure**: Satire posts must follow basic article presentation (a clear headline and a structured body with at least 2 distinct sentences/paragraphs). Single-line shitposts, unformatted chat screenshots without a parodic narrative and raw link drops are prohibited as low-effort spam.
- **Language & Content Rules**: All satire posts must be written in English (or include an English translation). Foreign profanity or toxic insults disguised as "satire" are strictly prohibited.
- **Rules Compliance**: Satire posts must strictly adhere to [General Server Rules](https://discord.com/channels/991752805213802516/1547670987913429022) and [Territorial.io Terms](https://territorial.io/terms). Foreign profanity, toxic insults, slurs or targeted harassment disguised as "satire" is strictly prohibited.
## 🤖 VII. AI-GENERATED CONTENT & LLM RULES
To maintain journalistic integrity and prevent low-effort spam, artificial intelligence (LLM) usage is regulated as follows:
- **Allowed Assistance**: Standard grammar tools, spellcheckers, translation software and formatting tools do not require disclosure.
- **Prohibited (Raw Slop)**: Publishing raw, unedited AI-generated drafts (e.g, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) is strictly prohibited as low-effort content. Articles must demonstrate genuine human drafting and editing effort.
- **Fact Verification & Liability**: Authors using AI for research or drafting remain **100% accountable for all factual claims, quotes and hallucinations**. Blaming an AI model will not mitigate misinformation strikes.
- **Mandatory AI Satire & Media Disclosure**: Articles utilizing AI-generated satire text or AI-generated media/fake screenshots MUST include this explicit footer:
`🤖 AI-generated content/satire`

## 🛡️ VIII. VERIFIED STATION STATUS
### 1. Territorial Journalist
The standard Discord role granted to approved News Station Owners and Journalists.
### 2. Verified Station
An earned recognition status awarded to news stations by News Managers that demonstrate:
- Consistent layout formatting and structural effort.
- Active responsiveness to News Manager feedback.
- A clean record with zero major policy strikes.
### 3. Priority Queueing
Verified Stations receive priority placement on the waiting list whenever a station slot opens up.

## 🛃 IX. EARLY WARNINGS, STRIKES & ENFORCEMENT
To promote constructive improvement over immediate punishment, News Managers operate an **Early Warning "Fix It" Window** before formal strikes are applied.
### ⏳ Early Warning Grace Periods
* 🟠 **Minor Issues**: **8 Hours** to edit and resolve the issue after receiving manager feedback.
* 🔴 **Major Issues**: **1 Hour** to edit and resolve the issue after receiving manager feedback.
* ⚫ **Severe Issues**: **0 Hours** (Immediate enforcement).

### ⚖️ Progressive Strike System (If Unfixed)
* 🟠 **Minor Offenses** *(e.g., missing clan tags, broken formatting, thread spam, minor factual typos)*:
  * **1st Offense**: Logged warning with constructive feedback.
  * **2nd Offense**: 3-day posting cooldown.
  * **3rd Offense**: 7-day probation.

* 🔴 **Major Offenses** *(e.g., undisclosed clan bias, fake news/misinformation, recruitment spam, uncredited AI hallucinations)*:
  * **1st Offense**: 7-day restriction.
  * **2nd Offense**: 14-day restriction + content review prior to returning.
  * **3rd Offense**: Permanent revocation of news publishing privileges.

* ⚫ **Severe Offenses** *(e.g., slurs, targeted harassment, malicious leaks, deliberate toxicity)*:
  * **Immediate Action**: 30-day revocation of station privileges and escalation to Admin Assistants (AA) for full server review.

> **Appeals & Transparency**: All actions are documented in private log threads and forwarded to their dedicated public news channels. Stations receiving a strike or removal have **7 days to file an appeal**. Appeals are reviewed by neutral representatives from the **Department of Justice (DoJ)** to ensure News Managers do not judge their own decisions.

## 📦 X. SLOT LIMITS & INACTIVITY
1. **15-Channel Cap**: The channel limit of 15 news station slots in [Territorial News](https://discord.com/channels/991752805213802516/1546928898460352573) remains in place to respect Discord channel limits.
2. **Inactivity Purge**: Stations inactive for **14 consecutive days** without publishing relevant news may have their slot reassigned to the top candidate on the waiting list.
3. **Easy Re-Application**: Stations removed purely due to inactivity may reapply immediately as soon as a slot opens up.

**CORE GUIDING PRINCIPLE:** We do not judge news stations based on who runs them; we judge them strictly by what they produce and how they act.

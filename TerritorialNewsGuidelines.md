# 📰 TERRITORIAL.IO NEWS GUIDELINES

## 1. FACTS, OPINION & REQUIRED LABELS

- Verify facts before publishing and support major factual claims with context links or screenshots. Keep coverage relevant to Territorial.io, clan politics, community events, updates or community tools.
- **NEWS:** Factual reporting. 
- **ANALYSIS:** Clearly identified interpretation grounded in facts or evidence.
- **EDITORIAL:** Opinion pieces must include:

`EDITORIAL — This article contains the opinion of its author or news station.`

**UNVERIFIED REPORT:** Rumors or materially unverified allegations must carry this disclaimer directly below the title:

`⚠️ UNVERIFIED REPORT: This article covers community rumors/speculation. No official evidence has been verified.`

Labels do not excuse fabricated claims, harassment or reckless disregard for accuracy.

Confidential sources and responsible public-interest leaks are allowed. Assess sources and corroborate material claims. Doxxing, passwords, intimate material and leaks enabling harassment or unauthorized access are prohibited.

Material does not become a Malicious Leak merely because it originated from a private, staff-only or archived channel. Applicable Server Rules concerning privacy or confidentiality still apply, and enforcement relying on such a rule must identify the specific rule.

## 2. RIGHT OF RESPONSE & CORRECTIONS

- For serious allegations, controversial disputes or major clan developments, offer affected parties a reasonable opportunity to comment where circumstances permit. You do not need their approval to publish.
- If contacted parties decline or do not respond, state:

`Reached out to [Party] for comment; no response was provided prior to publication.`

- Breaking news may proceed before a response arrives. Add, fairly summarize or acknowledge substantive later responses when reasonably practicable.
- Fix material factual errors promptly with a visible correction note. Retract reports whose central claim is fundamentally false or unsupported. Minor typos may be silently fixed.
- Good-faith errors are not automatically misinformation. Reckless misinformation and deliberate fabrication are Major offenses; fabrication causing serious harassment or safety risks may be Severe.
- Corrections may mitigate enforcement but do not automatically excuse reckless or deliberate misconduct.

## 3. CONDUCT, LINKS & PRESENTATION

- Lawful criticism, negative coverage and disagreement are protected. Retaliation, including selective enforcement or queue/Verified discrimination, is prohibited.
- No slurs, threats, harassment, bad-faith slander or provocation primarily intended to create personal conflict.
- Malicious Leaks are Severe offenses and receive immediate enforcement.
- No direct ads, recruitment campaigns or giveaway spam. Neutral community-event reporting and relevant links to safe, open-source community tools are allowed.
- **Independent stations:** One small masked footer link to their archive or Discord server.
- **Clan-affiliated stations:** No archive, publication-server or invite links, including indirect workarounds.
- Follow the Styling Guide: clear headers, readable spacing, sound English grammar and evidence for major claims.
- Removal solely for structural faults requires two News Managers. AAs may fill unavailable positions; two-person approval remains required.
- For Minor or Major structural faults, the article normally remains published during the applicable Fix-It Window so the journalist can correct it. Immediate temporary removal is allowed where continued publication creates a reasonable moderation, safety, privacy or substantial misinformation risk.
- If the structural issue is corrected within the deadline, the article must remain or be restored and no strike is issued for that corrected structural issue.

## 4. CLAN AFFILIATION & COVERAGE

Every article from a clan-affiliated station must include:

`⚠️ This news station is affiliated with [Clan Name].`

- No more than **4 of the latest 10 articles** may primarily concern the station's affiliated clan. Below 10 articles: no more than half, rounded up, capped at 4.
- Include the current article in the count. Incidental clan mentions do not count. Substance matters more than an article's title or label.
- Filler, artificial splitting and duplicate reports cannot be used to manipulate the count. Recruitment remains prohibited.
- Accidentally missing a tag is Minor; deliberately concealing affiliation is Major.

## 5. AI & SATIRE

- AI assistance is allowed. Raw, substantially unedited AI drafts are prohibited as low-effort content. Authors remain responsible for facts, quotes, sources and media.
- AI detectors alone are insufficient evidence of a violation. Grammar, translation and formatting tools need no disclosure.
- AI-generated satire text, synthetic media or fake AI screenshots require:

`🤖 AI-generated content/satire`

Satire belongs exclusively in **Satire News**. Include a headline, at least two body sentences, and English text or an English translation.

No single-line shitposts, raw link drops or screenshots without a parodic narrative. Slurs, harassment and foreign profanity do not become acceptable as satire.

Server Rules and Territorial.io Terms still apply.

## 6. DISCUSSION THREADS

One thread per article, directly beneath it, with the same title. No standalone, duplicate or off-topic threads.

Threads may be moderated for seven consecutive days without activity, toxicity, spam, bots or rule violations. Authors need not answer every reader; silence alone is not misconduct. Repeated thread spam may cost thread privileges.

## 7. WARNINGS, PENALTIES & APPEALS

**Fix-It windows after feedback:** Minor 8 hours; Major 1 hour; Severe immediate.

Remediable Minor or Major compliance issues corrected within the applicable Fix-It Window normally receive no strike for the corrected defect.

The Fix-It Window does not automatically erase responsibility for completed Major misconduct such as reckless misinformation, deliberate fabrication, deliberate concealment of affiliation or recruitment/advertising violations. Prompt correction may mitigate enforcement.

- **Minor, if unfixed:** Logged warning → 3-day posting cooldown → 7-day probation.
- **Major:** 7-day restriction → 14-day restriction + content review → permanent publishing revocation.
- **Severe:** Immediate 30-day station-privilege revocation + AA server review.

Conflicted managers must recuse. Disputed Major/Severe classifications require second review; immediate protection is allowed in Severe cases. AAs act when News Managers cannot.

Enforcement notice must identify the rule, reason, action, applicable correction deadline and appeal deadline.

Appeal directly to the **DoJ Tribunal within 7 days of notice**, including for article removal or Verified revocation. Enforcement remains active during appeal. Overturned actions must be cleared from disciplinary records and cannot harm future enforcement, queue treatment or Verified status.

## 8. STATION SLOTS & VERIFIED STATUS

- **15 slots:** A slot may be reassigned after 14 consecutive days without relevant news. Stations removed only for inactivity may reapply immediately through the normal queue.
- **Waiting list:** Verified applicants first, then standard applicants; first-come-first-served within each tier, using complete application time.
- **Verified:** Consistent quality, good-faith responsiveness to feedback and no Major strikes on record, excluding overturned strikes. Disagreement or appeals are not non-responsiveness.
- Grant/revocation requires two News Managers; AAs may substitute. Revocation needs written reasons and allows DoJ appeal.

Amendments require AA approval, public documentation and an effective date. New or stricter rules cannot be applied retroactively.

> We judge stations by what they produce and how they act, not who runs them.

> The same principle applies to enforcement: decisions must be based on written rules, evidence and consistent procedure; not identity, affiliation, personal disagreement or embarrassment caused by lawful reporting.
